# Code of conduct

Synapse follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, pull requests,
discussions or anywhere else it is represented, is expected to make it a respectful and
harassment-free experience for everybody.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Respect differing opinions, experience levels and backgrounds.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Reporting

If something happens that breaks this code, contact the maintainer,
[@atharv1205](https://github.com/atharv1205), privately through the contact details on
their GitHub profile. Every report will be reviewed and kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that do not
follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is
harmful, as set out in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
